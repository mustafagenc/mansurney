## Introduction

The reed that is the ney's main material is cut from the banks of the Orontes river in our province of Hatay. A drying process of about 6 months is applied. During this drying process, the reeds are hung with a heavy object tied to their bottoms. Care is taken that this place is cool. After the drying process, the reeds' proper measurements are determined and levelling begins. Any curve in the reed is straightened; using steam for this is better. After the headpiece is fitted, the pitch of the neys is determined with a tuning device. After this is determined, the boring (opening) of the ney is carried out. After the ney is bored, it is oiled. From this stage on, ney players must maintain their own neys.

## Step by step

1. The reed that is the ney's main material is cut from the banks of the Orontes river in our province of Hatay.
2. During a drying process of about 6 months, the reeds are hung with a heavy object tied to their bottoms. Care is taken that this place is cool.
3. After drying, the reeds' proper measurements are determined and levelling begins. Any curve in the reed is straightened; using steam for this is better.
4. The headpiece is fitted.
5. The pitch of the neys is determined with a tuning device.
6. After this is determined, the boring (opening) of the ney is carried out.
7. After the ney is bored, it is oiled.
