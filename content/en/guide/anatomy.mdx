## The headpiece (başpare)

The başpare is the piece placed on the mouth end of the ney, made from various woods or horns, that helps with blowing.

## The parazvane rings

Placed on the lower and upper ends of the ney to prevent cracking, this is material made of brass or alpaca (nickel silver).

## Joints and holes

Technically, the ney consists of nine joints and seven holes, six of them at the front. It is believed that these nine joints are in fact inspired by the nine-jointed human throat, and the seven holes by the human head — the ears, eyes, nose and mouth.
